Why tiers map to autonomy, not features

MIZ OKI 3.5 separates proposal from authorization. Every tier senses, reasons, plans, and validates the same way — the tier decides where the human gate sits. Starter keeps a person in every loop. Scale grants bounded autonomy inside thresholds you set. Enterprise runs the full ladder with per-scope grants, and some scopes stay forbidden to autonomy at every tier — by construction, not by policy document.
