§02 · Rotation

Four ways to swap, matched to severity.

Immediate

Critical decay or negative-feedback spikes: the creative is replaced outright. Audience trust is the asset being protected.

Gradual

Traffic shifts to the replacement in steps, letting delivery re-learn without the reset penalty of a hard swap.

Holdback

90% moves to the new creative; 10% stays on the old one as a live comparison (operating default) — the rotation itself is measured, not assumed.

Thompson-sampled

Replacement candidates are chosen by sampling from each creative's performance posterior — exploration weighted by evidence, with a freshness bonus.

Replacements pass a semantic-diversity check before serving: a near-duplicate of a fatigued creative inherits its fatigue, so near-duplicates are refused.

§03 · Learning

Every impression updates what earns the slot.

Adaptive experiments

Creative tests run as bandits — Thompson sampling by default, upper-confidence-bound where regret guarantees matter — so learning reallocates impressions continuously instead of waiting out a fixed A/B calendar.

Composite quality

Creatives are scored on relevance, engagement, freshness, differentiation from the rest of the pool, and brand safety — a weighted composite, with brand safety as a floor rather than a weight.

Stated plainly: triggers and weights on this page are operating defaults, not promised outcomes. Whether a rotation actually recovered performance is settled by the holdback comparison and written to the credit ledger.
