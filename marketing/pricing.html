// MIZ OKI 3.5 PRICING · transparent by design

Priced by autonomy, not by seats.

Every tier runs the same engine — the same 7-Stage Governed Decision System, the same Safety Guardrail Engine, the same Compounding ROI Memory. What changes is where the human gate sits. Pricing is scoped to the divisions you run, decision volume, and governance posture.

What we say

No sticker price, on purpose — scoped quotes against your divisions and volume, and the fastest way to scope one is to run the live demos first.

What we never say

Per-seat games, surprise overage mechanics, or a performance number as a promise. Targets are labeled targets, everywhere on this site.

// Three tiers, three autonomy modes

Start observed. Earn bounded. Graduate to full.

The tiers map one-to-one onto the autonomy modes on the Governance page — because how much rope you grant the system is the real pricing axis.

Starter · Observe Mode

Core Intelligence

One division, advisor-only. Every proposal routes to a human gate with the full decision trace and provenance chain. A scoped 90-day pilot with defined exit criteria.

Scale · Bounded Autonomy

Operational Autonomy

Multi-division on one shared memory. Low-risk moves execute inside thresholds you set; elevated moves route to Slack or Teams for 1-click approval. Full API access and decision replay.

Enterprise · Full Autonomy

Full Governance Suite

Org-wide, per-scope autonomy grants with instant rollback triggers, the Enterprise Privacy & Security Shield, dedicated environments, and forbidden scopes enforced by construction.

Some scopes stay forbidden to autonomy at every tier — by construction, not by policy document. A plan that fails the Safety Guardrail Engine is vetoed at every tier, including the highest.
