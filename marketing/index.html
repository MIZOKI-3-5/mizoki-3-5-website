00 / FOUR LOOPS · ONE CONTROLLER

Every signal enters a loop.
No loop outranks governance.

Evidence, causal measurement, decision control, and independently observed outcomes converge inside the seven-stage SRPVDAL Decision Control Plane.

L1

Evidence + intent

Consented first-party events, provenance, quality, identity boundaries, and expiring session evidence. Gate: consent fail-closed and O-1 privacy lock.

L2

Causal measurement

Treatment assignment, qualified holdouts, CATE, refutation, uncertainty, and calibration. Forecasts never grade their own incremental effect.

L3

Decision + passport

Ranked hypotheses, chosen and rejected plans, DEL gates, approval, rollback, dispatch or named veto—packaged as a ValidationPassport.

L4

Outcome + learning

Predicted and independently realized outcomes reconcile into decision memory. Activation requires a registered holdout.

One controller: SENSE → REASON → PLAN → VALIDATE → DECIDE → ACT → LEARN. Prediction never grades itself. A registered holdout is required before activation. A score cannot skip Validate or Decide; no-action always remains eligible.

03 / DECISION EVIDENCE CONTRACT

Ten questions before
any action.

This is a decision-evidence lens applied after the versioned canonical event schema—not a replacement for that schema. It turns normalized evidence into an auditable decision record.

01

What changed?

Record the observed physical, digital, or economic state change with source time and provenance.

02

Which entities?

Bind the affected campaign, landing page, SKU, system, and identity boundary without silent stitching.

03

What may explain it?

Rank a named, refutable causal hypothesis and preserve plausible alternatives and uncertainty.

04

Which objective?

Declare the financial or operating outcome, population, and time horizon before choosing a move.

05

Which policy applies?

Attach consent, authority, budget, margin, legal, security, and action-class constraints.

06

What could we do?

Generate a bounded candidate set that always includes hold, investigate, and no-action.

07

What could go wrong?

Expose downside, counterfactual uncertainty, blast radius, reversibility, and rollback readiness.

08

What did we predict?

Store the ex-ante outcome distribution, assumptions, model version, calibration, and abstention state.

09

What occurred?

Measure the ex-post outcome against the same definition, population, and horizon used before action.

10

What must change?

Update decision memory only from qualified evidence, retaining the prior trace and version history.

GATE 02Policy alignment
GATE 03Context sufficiency
GATE 04Hard constraints

08 / CAUSAL CREDIT

Forecasting is not incrementality.

Signal keeps three questions separate: what outcome may occur, who is likely to receive a treatment, and what the treatment changes. Only qualified treatment-effect evidence can support incremental-value claims.

SESSION OUTCOME FORECASTAdvisory probabilityCalibration · drift · abstention
TREATMENT ASSIGNMENTExperiment eligibilityConsent · exclusion · support
TREATMENT EFFECTQualified incrementalityEstimator selection · refutation · holdout
DECISION SETNo-action remains eligible.Risk · budget · policy · authority
QUALIFIED EXPERIMENT

Estimate one defined intervention.

Use as an anchor only when assignment, power, contamination, and outcome integrity support the population and test window.

HETEROGENEOUS EFFECTS

Estimate where effects may vary.

Select S-, T-, X-, or doubly robust approaches by assignment, overlap, imbalance, sample support, and diagnostics. No learner is uniformly best.

AGGREGATE MIX MODEL

Inform the longer allocation horizon.

Calibrate aggregate channel estimates against qualified experiments where definitions align; never present MMM as user-level causal truth.

Triangulation rule: align the treatment, outcome, population, and time horizon before reconciling results. Never average incompatible estimands. When qualified evidence conflicts, abstain and investigate.

08B / NET YIELD · IN BUILD

Revenue is not contribution.

Preview · in development. The economics layer is designed to preserve missingness instead of inventing profitability.

Observed revenue→Source transaction evidence with order and SKU lineage

Fulfillment + required costs→Missing required costs leave a row incomplete and excluded; costs are never invented
Net contribution evidence→Recommendation input only · NET_YIELD_WRITEBACK remains OFF

Control boundary: no claim that the platform currently bids on net contribution. Writeback requires its own verified pilot and separate approval.

10 / ADVANCED CONTROL FRONTIERS · IN BUILD

Five expansions.
Five different gates.

These frontiers have advanced from roadmap to governed implementation. They are not live capabilities, customer results, permission to act, or evidence that an activation gate has cleared.

F1IN BUILD · PROVISIONAL

Creative component effects

Embeddings describe promotion framing, layout, copy length, and imagery. Only randomized or otherwise qualified variation may identify causal effects.

Every estimate remains provisional until configured volume is met. Generated creative always requires human approval.
F2IN BUILD · DATA-GATED

Multi-quarter value

Balance immediate conversion against discounted contribution margin and repeat purchase across declared horizons.

Publish only with at least two observed quarters per cohort; otherwise return DATA_INSUFFICIENT.
F3IN BUILD · OBSERVE-ONLY

Inventory + fulfillment

Stock, safety thresholds, holding cost, and regional capacity may enter the graph as recommendation evidence.

No F3-originated action can reach a platform adapter, regardless of flags.
F4IN BUILD · PER-ACTION APPROVAL

Micro-geo calibration

Owner-configured aggregate geographies, exclusions, donors, and caps may support a bounded reservation proposal.

Every reservation halts for Level 2 approval. Two clean cycles plus an owner-approved config change are required before bounded-authority eligibility.
F5IN BUILD · CONFIG-DECLARED

Treasury-gated spend

Validated liquidity floors, covenant limits, and tightening curves may become named hard constraints in Validate and Decide.

Missing or invalid configuration means NOT_CONFIGURED—never an invented constraint or silent default.

Expansion workflow: merged artifact → registry verification → synthetic replay → qualified pilot → policy, security, and rollback review → separately approved account × action-class authority. MEASUREMENT_WRITEBACK and NET_YIELD_WRITEBACK remain OFF.

One brand. Limited, agreed source systems. Two consequential decision classes. Media, site, inventory, CRM, and margin inputs as available. Customer-specific evidence and economic baselines.
