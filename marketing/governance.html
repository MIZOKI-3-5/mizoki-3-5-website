// Governance & Security

Autonomy you dial in. Security by construction.

Autonomy is not a switch — it is a ladder you climb at your own pace, with the Safety Guardrail Engine enforcing the same policy arithmetic at every rung.

Observe Mode

Advisor only

MIZ OKI senses, diagnoses, and recommends — with the full evidence chain — and executes nothing. Every proposal waits for a human. The right first rung for every account.

Bounded Autonomy

Low-risk auto, high-risk approval

Minor, reversible moves inside your declared thresholds execute hands-free. Major budget moves route to Slack or Teams for 1-click approval. You set the thresholds; the engine cannot exceed them.

Full Autonomy

Safe rules, instant rollback

Declared-safe rules run end to end with instant rollback triggers armed on every action. Forbidden scopes stay forbidden at every level — by construction, not by policy document.

At every mode, a plan that fails the Safety Guardrail Engine — or loses to the "Do Nothing" Opportunity Cost Check — is vetoed. Nothing executes, and the veto is recorded to Compounding ROI Memory like any other decision.

// Enterprise Privacy & Security Shield

Your data. Your memory. Your keys.

The shield is structural — isolation is how the system is built, not a setting someone has to remember to turn on.

Tenant memory isolation

Your Compounding ROI Memory is yours alone. What the loop learns from your accounts never trains, tunes, or leaks into anyone else's.

Customer-managed keys

Customer-managed keys (CMEK) are a design target on the enterprise roadmap — encryption at rest is on by default, and access ends when you say it ends.

No cross-account bleed

Account boundaries are enforced in the data path itself — by construction, not by convention.

Tamper-evident audit trails

Every decision, approval, veto, and dispatch is recorded append-only, with replay — the trail shows what happened and cannot be quietly rewritten.

Governance is the product.

The deliberate red veto in every demo is not a bug reel — it is the pitch. Watch the guardrails hold, then decide how much rope to grant.
