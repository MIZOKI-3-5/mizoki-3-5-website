// Channel Intelligence Modules

Specialist depth, per channel. One governed loop.

Each module speaks its channel's native dialect — auctions, pixels, SKUs, inboxes — and feeds the same 7-Stage Governed Decision System. Modules are how MIZ OKI grows: a new channel, or an entirely new operating domain, onboards as a new module on the same loop. The four below are the initial media-buying set.

E-Commerce & Inventory Module
