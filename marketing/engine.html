// The Engine

The 7-Stage Governed Decision System.

Every campaign action — from a $40 bid nudge to a full budget reallocation — walks the same seven governed stages. This is the whole loop, in media-buyer terms, exactly as the simulator and the live desks run it.

Sense
Reason
Plan
Validate
Decide
Act
Learn
01 Sense Full-Stack Radar

Real-time monitoring across Meta, Google Ads, Shopify, edge servers, and stock levels. Every reading lands as Structured Signal Evidence — one format, timestamped, provenance attached, cross-checked against its source system before anyone reasons on it.

watches ad networks · web stack · commerce · inventory · finance guardrails

02 Reason Root Cause AI

Connects cause and effect over time — distinguishing true creative fatigue from site lag or pixel sync delay. The Cross-Stack Root Cause Engine correlates evidence across the systems your tools treat as silos and names the driver with a confidence figure attached.

answers "why did CPA move?" · output named cause + confidence + evidence chain

03 Plan Actionable Strategy

Auto-generates the specific move: bid shifts, negative keyword lists, or spend reallocations — sized to the diagnosed impact, not to a hunch. Channel Intelligence Modules contribute the channel-specific tactics; the loop composes one strategy.

produces bid shifts · negative keywords · reallocations · pauses

04 Validate Safety Brakes

The Safety Guardrail Engine verifies target ROAS floors, daily spend caps, brand rules, and margin guardrails before anything is allowed to move — and every plan must pass the "Do Nothing" Opportunity Cost Check: action has to beat inaction on the evidence. A plan that fails is vetoed. The veto is arithmetic, not an opinion.

gates on ROAS floors · spend caps · brand rules · margins · failure mode veto — nothing executes

05 Decide 1-Click Approvals

Minor tweaks inside your autonomy grants execute automatically. Major budget moves route to Slack or Teams for one-click approval — you commit the decision with the full evidence chain in front of you.

low risk auto-approve inside grants · elevated routed for 1-click sign-off

06 Act Hands-Free Execution

Pushes live bid, budget, and campaign updates directly to the ad platforms — every dispatch logged against the decision that authorized it. No copy-paste, no 11 p.m. console sessions.

dispatch via Google Ads API · Meta API · commerce APIs · every call logged to its decision

07 Learn Compounding ROI Memory

Remembers historical campaign results — what was decided, why, and what happened next — permanently. Future ad decisions start faster and smarter, whoever is on shift.

records decision + rationale + outcome · effect compounding judgment
