// The contract, in plain terms

What it watches. What it decides. What it never does.

Every division runs the same 7-Stage Governed Decision System, under the same Safety Guardrail Engine, writing to the same Compounding ROI Memory. Here is Counsel's slice of it — stated plainly, so you can hold us to it.

Watches
  • Contracts, amendments, and side letters
  • Renewal and notice windows
  • Counterparty correspondence
  • Dispute posture and exposure
Decides
  • Obligation and deadline alerts
  • Renewal recommendations with the clause quoted
  • Conflict flags — synthesis stops until a human clears them
  • Escalations to human counsel
Never does
  • Signs, files, or sends anything external
  • Advises beyond its evidence
  • Clears its own conflicts
