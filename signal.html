§02 · SRPVDAL

From signal to governed action.

One closed control loop. Seven accountable stages. No shortcut from prediction to execution.

  1. SENSE

    Capture consented, provenance-bearing first-party interaction telemetry. No consent, no event.

  2. REASON

    Forecast defined outcomes and form temporary, uncertainty-bearing intent hypotheses.

  3. PLAN

    Generate message, creative, timing, or no-action candidates — never direct activation.

  4. VALIDATE

    Test consent, quality, policy, calibration, causality, risk, and rollback. Non-bypassable.

  5. DECIDE

    Compare expected incremental value, uncertainty, budget, and downside risk. iROAS, not platform ROAS.

  6. ACT

    Execute only through the Decision Control Plane's governed execution adapters, after every gate passes and the exact mutation is authorized.

  7. LEARN

    Reconcile verified outcomes, recalibrate, expire hypotheses, preserve evidence.

O-1 · Privacy locked

What the system refuses to learn matters.

Keystroke dynamics are excluded from ingestion permanently — alongside the standing bright lines: no audio, no gaze, no real-time location. Rejection happens at the schema, not in policy documents.
