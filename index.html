§03HOW IT WORKS

Every signal travels one governed pathway.

Every MIZ OKI Media decision moves through the platform's seven-stage operating loop — SRPVDAL — in this exact order, and every stage leaves a record. Nothing executes until it is validated against your constraints and authorized by the right person or the right delegated rule.

01

Sense

Detect meaningful commercial change — media, commerce, site, inventory, and economic thresholds, normalized into evidence with provenance.

02

Reason

Evaluate competing hypotheses for why performance moved, with the supporting and disconfirming evidence preserved.

03

Plan

Generate multiple possible interventions — including doing nothing — and compare their expected outcomes.

04

Validate

Apply organizational constraints: finance, margin, inventory, brand, compliance, and risk. Vetoes are recorded, never silent.

05

Decide

Route the recommendation to the correct authority — a named person, a delegated rule, or a bounded automation.

06

Act

Execute only the authorized action — scoped, bounded, with its rollback path preserved. "No action" is a first-class outcome.

07

Learn

Compare predicted with realized outcomes and write the adjustment into memory, so the next decision starts from a smarter baseline.
