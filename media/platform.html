Platform

The Commercial Decision Operating System

MIZ OKI Media is not another reporting platform. Reporting tells you what happened. An operating system governs what happens next: it holds the evidence, weighs the causes, tests the responses, enforces the rules, routes the authority, executes within bounds, and learns from the result.

Seven capabilities. One continuous operating model. Connected by the Decision Graph.

Seven capabilities

What the system does

Each capability is a stage of the same governed pathway — not a separate module bolted on. Remove any one and the decision stops being governable.

01 · feeds SENSE

Commercial Intelligence

One structured evidence stream across media, commerce, experience, inventory, and economics — with provenance, timestamps, and quality attached to every observation. Not a dashboard: an evidentiary record.

02 · drives REASON

Causal Reasoning

Competing explanations are tested against the evidence — supporting and disconfirming — instead of assuming the loudest metric is the cause. The reasoning itself is preserved, not just the conclusion.

03 · drives PLAN

Counterfactual Planning

Every candidate action is compared against alternatives — including doing nothing. The expected effect and its assumptions are stated up front, so the outcome can be honestly scored later.

04 · is VALIDATE

Policy Validation

Financial floors, brand rules, inventory limits, risk boundaries, and compliance constraints are applied to every candidate action — and every pass, fail, and veto is recorded, never silent.

05 · is DECIDE

Human Approval

Recommendations route to the authority that actually owns them: a named person, a delegated rule, or a bounded automation grant. Autonomy exists only where it was explicitly given.

06 · is ACT

Governed Execution

Only the authorized action dispatches — scoped, bounded, reversible, and logged. "No action" is a first-class outcome, recorded with the same rigor as action.

07 · is LEARN

Outcome Learning

Predicted and realized outcomes are compared on every decision. Calibration is adjusted, weighting is updated, and the next decision starts from a smarter baseline. Memory compounds.
