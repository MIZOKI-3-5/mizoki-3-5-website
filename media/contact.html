What helps us scope quickly

Your stack, roughly

The media platforms, commerce systems, and analytics you run today. Names are enough — no credentials, no exports, nothing sensitive.

What happens next

No pressure, by design

First reply
A short set of scoping questions — the ones on this page, sharpened for your context.
Scoping conversation
One working session to pick the two pilot decision classes, the evidence boundaries, and the authority map.
Proposal
The 90-day plan with its success metrics, governance checkpoints, and exit criteria in writing — including the option to stop at day 90 and keep everything the pilot learned.

We don't do countdown timers or expiring offers. The pilot's exit criteria are the sales pitch: if the record doesn't justify expanding, you'll be the first to see that — in the scorecard we hand you.
