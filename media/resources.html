Frequently asked questions

Straight answers

Is this another dashboard or BI tool?

No. Dashboards describe movement; MIZ OKI Media governs the response — it diagnoses the cause, tests the options against your policies, routes the decision to its owner, executes only what was authorized, and scores the result. Keep your dashboards; they remain useful for describing.

Does the AI act on its own?

Only inside authority that a human explicitly granted, and the default everywhere is observe-only. Everything else routes to a named approver. Autonomy never expands by itself — every increase is a recorded human decision, and rollback authority always stays human.

What data does it need?

The pilot scopes a limited set of media and commerce sources plus the constraints that govern them — margin floors, budget ceilings, inventory position. Evidence boundaries are explicit: what may be observed, at what granularity, with what identity resolution, is agreed at scoping.

Does it replace our existing stack?

No. Warehouses keep the facts, CDPs keep the profiles, platforms keep their automation. The Decision Graph keeps the decisions — the layer none of the others were built to hold. It reads from your stack and complements it.

How fast do we see value?

The pilot is built to answer that honestly rather than promise it in advance: observe-only for the first thirty days, recommendations under human review in the second thirty, limited governed actions in the third — with predicted-versus-realized measured throughout. The scorecard tells you what the system earned.

What does it cost?

Pilot pricing is scoped with you around the two decision classes, the sources involved, and the governance work required — discussed directly, not published as a one-size number. The exit criteria mean you are never paying for a default renewal.

Are you certified for compliance frameworks?

We do not claim certifications on this site. We name our security and privacy principles — least-privilege access, role-based authorization, consent-first evidence boundaries, auditable records — and review them against your requirements during pilot scoping.

What is the relationship to MIZ OKI?

MIZ OKI Media is a focused commercial application powered by the broader MIZ OKI Operating Knowledge Intelligence platform — one product surface on the same governed decision infrastructure, not a separate company and not a separate platform.

What happens after the pilot?

One of three explicit exits: expand along a named roadmap, extend with defined gaps and a firm end date, or stop — keeping the scorecard, the decision records, and everything the pilot learned about your stack. Expansion is a decision you make on the record, never a default.
