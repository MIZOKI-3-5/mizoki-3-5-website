§02 · Under the hood, in plain English

Four moving parts. Zero jargon required.

Structured Signal Evidence

Every event from every system — ad platforms, web stack, analytics, inventory — normalized into one clean, comparable record. Evidence you can line up side by side, whatever stack produced it.

Cross-Stack Root Cause Engine

The diagnosis layer. It tests candidate causes against each other across your whole stack and names the true driver of a metric move — with the receipts attached.

Channel Intelligence Modules

Specialist operators for each surface — search, social, commerce, lifecycle — that know their channel's levers and act only inside the shared safety envelope.

Compounding ROI Memory

Every decision and its measured outcome, recorded permanently. The system's judgment compounds week over week — and the record never forgets, never rewrites.

§03 · The 7-Stage Decision Control System

One governed loop, from raw signal to booked outcome.

Sense, Reason, Plan, Validate, Decide, Act, Learn. Seven stages between "something moved" and "the right thing happened about it" — every one of them inspectable, every one of them governed.

01SENSE24/7 Full-Stack Radar

Monitors Google Ads, Meta, Shopify, and your infrastructure around the clock. Every event lands as Structured Signal Evidence — one clean, comparable record per signal — so a page-speed regression and a CPM shift can sit in the same evidence file.

02REASONRoot Cause AI

Identifies why a metric fluctuated — a site-speed drop versus creative fatigue — by testing candidate causes against the Cross-Stack Root Cause Engine instead of eyeballing charts. The verdict names a driver, not a vibe.

03PLANActionable Strategy

Generates the concrete move: bid shifts, budget reallocations, or campaign pauses — sized, sequenced, and costed. A plan here is an executable instruction, not a slide.

04VALIDATESafety Brakes

Checks every plan against budget limits, brand rules, and margin thresholds before it can touch a dollar. A plan that fails here is stopped, not softened — the veto is a first-class outcome.

05DECIDE1-Click Approvals

Low-risk tweaks clear automatically inside your policy envelope. Anything bigger routes to Slack or Teams for a one-click human decision — with the evidence and the projected outcome attached.

06ACTHands-Free Execution

Pushes approved changes directly through the ad-platform APIs. No tab-switching, no copy-paste, no 2 a.m. bid babysitting — and every dispatch is logged with what it changed.

07LEARNCompounding ROI Memory

Records what each action actually did, so the next decision starts smarter than the last. Outcomes feed back into diagnosis and planning — the system's judgment compounds, and the ledger never forgets.
